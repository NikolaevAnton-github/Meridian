# HeightCorrection04 spatial trial - rejected for insufficient visual improvement

These HLSL files and recipe describe the single archived spatial trial, not the
current live material. HC-R1 remained unresolved in close, lateral and true-input
hold views. The trial native packages are preserved under
`Saved/OpeningLobby/UpperVoid01/HeightCorrection04/Worker/FailedTrial/`.

The final map and both dedicated atmosphere assets were restored byte-for-byte
from `HeightCorrection04/Controller/BeforeCorrection/archive.json` to the
HeightCorrection03 identity. Its unchanged source remains in the sibling
`HeightCorrection03` directory. No source or evidence from earlier work was edited.

The field is static and smooth, with three oblique 3D wavelengths of approximately
3, 4 and 5 metres. Weighted values continuously modulate onset within 13.7-15.1 m
and density across each shaft. Sixteen midpoint samples integrate absorption
through the portion of the view ray above 13.7 m. The independent terminal mask
reaches zero transmission at 17.9 m. No lights, surfaces or geometry were changed.

This working interpretation of mild irregularity is not owner-approved design.
