## Circulation and sightlines

The two physical checkpoint openings across the entire 24 m hall width are
Y=-5.4..-3.9 and Y=3.9..5.4, each 1.5 m. The joined desk, lane posts and closed
terminal enclosures account for the rest of the floor-level control line.
This is a walking-route statement, not security gameplay or anti-jump design.

After either lane, the hall connects to the remaining side aisles through open
6 m bays centred at X=+/-16.8. These corridors occupy absolute Y 8..12 only
between the transverse caps. The entrance service approaches stop at X=-19.15;
inner hall approaches stop at absolute Y 4.95. These are closed endpoints with
0.65 m centre-to-face distance, not traversable interior destinations.

Verifier assumptions remain capsule radius 0.34, half-height 0.88, eye 1.72,
human 1.8 and walk 3.6 m/s. Sampling step is at most 0.025 m and half a sample
step is subtracted from the minimum distance for a conservative continuous-path
bound. Room gross envelopes are unavailable obstacles in addition to the
actual floor-contact drawing bounds. Both full checkpoint approaches, both
remaining aisles, both alternate crossings, all four room approaches and all
three passages at both new pairs are covered by 15 routes / 12,041 samples.

Nominal straight 60 m travel is 16.7 s. The actual lane approach from X=-29.3 to
the hall axis at -21 is 13.865 m / 3.851 s; continuing to X=28.8 adds 49.8 m /
13.833 s, for 63.665 m / 17.685 s. Each alternate-bay-to-bay aisle route is 53.6 m
including its two 10 m lateral crossings, or 14.889 s. An individual side passage
detour around a new pair is 15.2 m / 4.222 s. These are walk estimates, not game tests.

At eye height the centre-axis segment from [-19,0] to [29.39,0] is clear of
drawn solids. The segment from [-19,2.4] to the same elevator point intersects
the first positive-Y new shaft. The service-door approach sightline is clear
to the transverse face. The new columns deliberately interrupt lateral views;
technical route clearance does not establish that their density is visually
acceptable. Sheet 05C exposes that tradeoff against the unchanged main volume.
