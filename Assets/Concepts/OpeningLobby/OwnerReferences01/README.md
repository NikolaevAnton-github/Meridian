## Selected visual characteristics

- Long axial hall, symmetrical rows of large square stone columns and narrower
  flanking aisles.
- Heavy horizontal stone beams above the columns, enclosing a taller central
  space without decorative coffers or hotel-style ornament.
- Dark green-grey stone cladding and polished veined flooring; two parallel
  black strips define the central approach.
- Tall vertical glazing at an end wall and cool linear lighting along the
  side aisles. Preserve the austere green-grey tone and readable reflections.
- Sparse institutional security fittings in dark metal, intact surfaces and
  strict architectural repetition.

## Decision and open detail

The owner selected this visual direction; Review01-A/B/C are historical
alternatives, not the current target. The rejected Stage 1 room dimensions,
column spacing and three-elevator end wall are not constraints on this direction.

The owner confirmed on 2026-09-13 that these are opposite views of one hall:
the glazed entrance/checkpoint is at one end, the small opaque door at the other.
That earlier confirmation established image orientation, not exact dimensions,
off-camera connections or the function beyond the small door. At that point it
did not constitute final approval of the complete art package or authorization
to implement a 3D layout.

The owner subsequently explicitly approved `LobbyArt-Review02` on 2026-09-13:
these two unchanged images together with the
[Review02 oblique entrance security view](../Review02/README.md). This approval
satisfies the MSQ-8 owner approval gate and authorizes the separate
[layout revision](../../../../Docs/Tasks/OpeningLobbyLayout02.md). Preserve the
original reference pair unchanged; it remains authoritative where the supplement's
fine details differ. Exact dimensions, off-camera connections and the small door's
function remain unresolved.

The Stage 1 layout remains rejected. MSQ-6 detailed architecture/material
