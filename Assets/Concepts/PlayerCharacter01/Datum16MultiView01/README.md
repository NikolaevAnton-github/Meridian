# Datum 16: eight input-art views

Eight separate PNG illustrations of operative 16 / Datum on a white background,
prepared for the owner's multi-view input experiment. This is input-art
preparation only. Final owner selection and modeling approval remain pending.

Left and Right follow the owner's direction thumbnails. In the Left profile,
the nose points image-left and the wearer's left side is visible. In the Right
profile, the nose points image-right and the wearer's right side, including
the original temple computer housing, is visible. The same facing-direction
convention applies to the two 45-degree views.

Top and Bottom specify overhead and underfoot projections, respectively,
with the camera looking down from above the head or up from beneath the feet.
Strong foreshortening is expected in these projections.

## Scope and provenance

Rendered with built-in imagegen. Multica Concept Art prepared the view prompts
at Astra/max/standard; the controller normalized the Left/Right slot convention
to match the owner's thumbnails and used the new Front view as an additional
identity anchor. The original artist prompts retain their initial camera-position
convention; render-requests.json records the actual requests and corrections.
Two discarded Top attempts and one discarded Bottom attempt remain in Saved.
The final Top/Bottom drawings are compact axial reference illustrations; exact
orthographic geometry and equal projected scale across views are not established.

The original Concept02/16.png and prior Datum16TrellisInput01 artwork are
identity references. Prior protected package files passed SHA-256 comparison
against the controller's pre-task snapshot. This packaging script reads the
delivered PNGs without editing or resaving their pixels. The manifest records
their exact bytes, dimensions, modes and outer-border white statistics.

The images are generated illustrations. Hidden surfaces are inferred, and
three-dimensional consistency has not been verified. They are not a validated
production turnaround, mesh, topology, rig or deformation design. Border
statistics are technical observations, not independent art review or owner
acceptance. No independent art review or external 3D generation was performed
as part of this package.
