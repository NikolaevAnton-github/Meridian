# PlayerArt-Concept01 / Preparation01

MSQ-51 creative preparation, 2026-09-16. Fifteen alternative outfits for one
original protagonist; the numbers are identifiers, not a ranking. The names
describe proposed construction, not character names, ranks or MERIDIAN roles.
This package contains design proposals and rendering instructions. No images
have been rendered by the artist, and no visual selection has been made.

## Fixed basis

- The setting is **2043**, with a realistic, dark near-future tone.
- The protagonist belongs to MERIDIAN, a secret government unit of trained
  parautilitarians. Time slowdown, force push and telekinesis are player abilities;
  this brief does not assign them a technological origin.
- Following the catastrophe, the protagonist is isolated in the skyscraper
  without command contact. The cause of his survival is unresolved. Neither
  distinctive equipment nor costume damage establishes an explanation.
- Owner requirements: original full-body designs, entirely concealed faces,
  plausible mobile human equipment, and owner-only evaluation of actual images.
- Rifle use and readable gloves, sleeves, legs and boots matter to the planned
  first-person body. This batch does not design a weapon or validate a rig.

## Proposal boundaries

All masks, garment cuts, materials, protection, load carriage, palettes and body
presentation below are alternatives for owner selection. They establish no
uniform standard, unit insignia, rank, biography or new ability. Keep one plausible
adult human baseline across the batch; clothing creates the silhouette changes.
Protection descriptions express design intent, not certified ballistic performance.
Every lens is externally opaque to the viewer; no eyes, facial contours, hair,
ears, neck skin or wrist skin are visible. A plausible viewing lens remains part
of the mask rather than an unexplained blind faceplate.

Equipment has ordinary physical purposes. No power gauntlets, anomaly detectors,
exoskeletons, glowing systems or survival devices. Avoid prominent national,
military, franchise and manufacturer markings. Any stowed communications pouch
does not imply restored command contact. Unspecified identity and story details,
ability mechanics, final equipment specification and production dimensions
remain open. The outfit's light use wear does not depict a particular story event.

## Shared sheet direction

Use one clean landscape concept sheet per variant: a dominant full-body
three-quarter front view, a smaller coherent full-body rear view, and two insets
showing the closed worn mask and the worn glove with sleeve cuff. Keep the entire
head and both soles inside both full-body views. Give the rear view enough size
to read closures and load carriage. Hands stay empty and separated from the body.
Show no weapon, holster or weapon-design study.

Use a neutral warm-gray backdrop, soft directional studio light, readable dark
materials and a deliberate painterly finish. Distinguish cloth, rubber, leather
and composite through surface response and construction. Limited wear belongs
at cuffs, seams, knees and soles; avoid dramatic wounds or catastrophe storytelling.
The only principal sheet text is `MERIDIAN / 2043` and the stable variant number
and name. Keep names in English. No approval marks or insignia on clothing.

Leave shoulders, underarms, elbows and fingers mobile; keep the stock seating
area and cheek area low profile. Upper legs must bend, the coat/panel variants
must open above the thigh crease, and pouches must not block crouching or access
to the waist. These are visual design intentions awaiting later rig testing.

## Rendering and decision boundary

`prompts.json` is the rendering source. For each row, concatenate
`common_prompt + "\n\n" + variant.prompt` and submit one independent built-in
imagegen request. Include no previous images as references for these new designs.
Render serially. Preserve the exact submitted text and original returned image
bytes under this concept root. The controller supplies the gallery and immutable
image manifest, then checks delivery requirements without ranking or aesthetic
acceptance. The detailed handoff is
`Saved/PlayerCharacter01/Concept01/Artist/handoff.md`.

Fifteen actual delivered images are still required. Prompts alone do not complete
MSQ-51. Owner selection must identify the package/version/variant and exact image
before modeling; MSQ-53 is the owner-only selection gate. MSQ-52's animation audit
and rig contract remain prerequisites. This preparation dispatches neither task
and authorizes no model, weapon, gameplay or environment changes.
