# Art, environment and narrative policy

Read for relevant art/design work only. Original protagonist production is paused
and lobby architecture deferred; task history does not reopen either.

## Environment and visual acceptance

Before environment layout/model production, create a separate concept-art task
and obtain explicit owner approval of an identified art version. Preproduction
requires dimensioned plans/sections with human scale, approved as a named package
before renewed 3D work. Preserve approved scale; label dimensional changes as
proposals requiring owner review.

Follow [VisualAcceptance](../VisualAcceptance.md) within authorized scope. Independent
visual reviewers inspect actual art and comparable views. Technical validation,
independent review and owner visual/play acceptance are distinct. A prototype pass
or a next-task request does not approve a visual design.

Use [GameBrief](../Design/GameBrief.md) for game/level requirements and
[StoryCanon](../Design/StoryCanon.md) for narrative. Keep fixed facts, proposals and
unresolved details separate. The documented first playable is the
[lobby walkthrough](../Tasks/OpeningLobby.md), still subject to owner deferral.
For resumed lobby work, read [deferral/preservation](../OpeningLobbyDeferred01.md)
and the relevant retained asset acceptance before editing. Accepted UpperVoid
bytes, materials, owner geometry and history remain preserved.

## Original protagonist and arms

For this scope, consult the [task plan](../Tasks/PlayerCharacter01Plan.md), using
relevant sections. Earlier instructions also require
`Docs/Tasks/PlayerCharacter01Tasks.md` and `Docs/PlayerCharacter01AI3DPipeline.md`;
both are absent from this checkout at the 2026-09-24 audit. Recover/verify those
sources before resumed production; missing files do not waive their approval gates.
Canonical editable sources: `Assets/Source/PlayerCharacter01/`. Preserve all
concept packages and owner experiments. Named concept approval is required before
production modeling, including use of a concept as a planning example.

The owner alone evaluates the protagonist concept batch: do not assign an
independent concept reviewer. Later integrated review is separate. Existing
Tripo Studio/Meshy web allowances are the scoped generation-budget exception;
free Hunyuan3D Studio requires verified terms. No paid API/top-up/new subscription.
