# ED-01: First rifle-driven local fracture specimen

Multica issue: **MSQ-141**. Parent: [EnvironmentDestruction01 / MSQ-74](../EnvironmentDestruction01.md).
Draft prepared 2026-09-24; prepared only, no implementation dispatched.
Follow the [execution plan](../EnvironmentDestruction01Plan.md) and
[owner authority](../../Approvals/EnvironmentDestruction01-LobbyLab01.json).

## Objective and dependencies

After [ED-00](ED-00.md), place one explicitly identified, existing nonstructural
specimen in `/Game/Maps/L_OpeningLobby_DestructionLab01`. An actual rifle hit must
produce visible, spatially local damage and a bounded physical break at the hit
region, while the rest of the object remains meaningful geometry.

The ED-00 selection must identify the source, dimensions, material and intended
cover/cosmetic role. A wall, column, floor, ceiling, storefront or other retained
architectural asset is not an implicit specimen choice. Missing suitable sources
require a bounded asset decision, not new environment production.

## Work and deliverables

- Derive task-owned editable fracture/collision/material assets as needed and
  place a removable gameplay instance in the existing lab copy. Never mutate the
  shared source mesh, material or owner-authored packages.
- Connect the real rifle/projectile damage path to hit-position-based fracture.
  Record thresholds, mass, impulse and fragment limits as tunable parameters.
  Preserve the purchased-rifle baseline, finite-projectile launch safety and
  existing damage ownership; a debug-triggered break alone is insufficient.
- Produce a visible local damaged region and a small bounded detached fragment
  response. Record what remains intact and its collision/hit-blocking behavior.
  Whole-object disappearance or an unrelated canned replacement does not establish
  local damage. Accumulation and remaining connections are developed in ED-02.
- Provide a simple repeatable specimen reset and the exact shot route/controls for
  the next task. Keep temporary effects minimal; final impact presentation is ED-06.

## Acceptance and evidence

- Actual rifle impacts at two meaningfully separated positions affect their own
  local regions. An untouched region retains its geometry and intended blocking.
- Visible removal and collision/hit blocking agree for this first fracture state;
  no invisible intact proxy remains across the removed region. Detachment does not
  cause explosive impulse or an unbounded fragment count.
- The map/source preservation boundary from ED-00 still holds. Reset restores the
  same specimen without spawning accumulating duplicates.
- Save focused before/after views or short footage, hit location/damage records,
  resulting geometry/collision checks and a local cost observation under
  `Saved/EnvironmentDestruction01/ED-01/<Candidate>/`. Identify commit and relevant
  dirty files, map, actor, derived packages/fingerprints, tuning, runtime settings,
  evidence run and actual worker settings. Report untested criteria explicitly.

Use the plan's primary technical review and focused recheck route. This task does
not implement new AI behavior, whole-building damage or wall expansion.
