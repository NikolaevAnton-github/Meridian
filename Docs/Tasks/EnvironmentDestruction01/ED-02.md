## Objective and dependencies

Extend the identified [ED-01](ED-01.md) specimen in the same lab map. Repeated hits
accumulate in the affected region; surviving connections/support determine which
portions stay attached. Damage must create usable geometry changes, not merely a
visual overlay or a single object-wide health switch.

Extend the selected column to three functional regions: breakable cladding,
a bounded breakable concrete surface volume, and a permanent visible solid core.
Both outer materials must shed convincingly under local rifle damage; an always
pristine flat backing beneath removed cladding is no longer the target result.
The column cannot collapse or sever through, and the ceiling is unaffected.
The later clarification changes future scope, not delivered ED-01 evidence.
The controller selects one standalone thin panel derived from the existing ED-01
lower-course cladding recipe and unchanged face material for witness-target
pass-through evidence. Record candidate dimensions/placement before authoring;
the column cannot satisfy that criterion. This technical fixture is not the ED-04
second material or approval of new environment art.

## Work and deliverables

- Define a bounded damage/fracture representation suitable for this specimen.
  Record the local accumulation rule, fragment/connectivity limits and support
  assumptions. Reuse the existing damage path; avoid a general destruction-engine
  rewrite or simulation of structural building failure.
- Record the cladding/concrete damage volumes, maximum concrete loss depth and
  permanent residual-core envelope. Preserve the original outer dimensions and
  use task-owned lab derivatives. Exact depth, thresholds and fragment budgets are
  candidate tuning; do not claim owner-approved numeric values or exact ballistics.
- Produce irregular volumetric recesses and varied cladding/concrete fragments.
  Include inclined and uneven break faces and chips, with distinct face, fracture
  interior and exposed concrete treatments. Basic interior texture/relief and
  roughness belong here, not only in ED-06. Avoid a uniform second peeled shell
  at the depth limit; the retained visible boundary must still read as damaged.
- Retain physical world and mutual collision for large visible fragments of both
  materials, separately from weapon-query and Pawn rules. Do not retain ED-01's
  blanket PhysicsBody ignore as the default for such pieces. Preserve rifle safety
  guards and verify shots during debris motion. Fine cosmetic chips may be excluded
  explicitly. ED-03 extends this baseline to stable piles and bounded lifecycle.
- Distinguish clustered hits, spatially separated hits and removal of a remaining
  supporting connection. Retained pieces must have an explainable attachment;
  newly disconnected pieces must detach without unrelated sections disappearing.
- Update collision, rifle obstruction and the declared cover-validity state with
  geometry changes. Demonstrate a hole large enough for the declared shot test;
  only claim passage/traversal where the actual opening and collision allow it.
- Expose the existing adapter notification seam with stable specimen/revision
  identity and documented timing. Existing actual-geometry guards may establish
  this task's cover/obstruction result. New Utility/GOAP plan cancellation, cover
  selection and route requery belong to [UG-08 / MSQ-131](../UtilityGOAP01/UG-08.md).
  This task does not depend back on MSQ-131 or implement a parallel AI path.

## Acceptance and evidence

- A focused repeated-hit sequence shows local accumulation, while an untouched
  region remains intact. A separated-hit comparison demonstrates that damage is
  not simply one undifferentiated global threshold.
- One representative remaining-connection transition shows the expected attachment
  and detachment. Record the simplified physical assumptions and limits honestly.
- A focused actual-rifle sequence shows intact cladding, exposed concrete, local
  concrete loss and exhausted damage depth. Comparable front/oblique/close views
  show irregular depth, material interiors and both fragment types. Unhit regions
  remain intact; the final state must not read as a cleanly stripped smooth column.
- Continued hits at the depth limit do not sever, delete or collapse the column.
  Damage accumulation, including any represented edges/adjoining faces, cannot
  erode the declared permanent core. The core retains shot and body blocking.
  Shots hit the remaining visible core at the actual surface; no invisible old
  backing spans a recess and no witness target is reachable through the column.
- A representative pair/group of large cladding and concrete fragments collides
  and settles without persistent overlap or visible overlap flicker. Check normal
  time and canonical slowdown for changed damage/physics behavior. Record defects
  explicitly instead of relying on future dust to conceal them.
- Actual rifle shots are blocked by surviving solid geometry and pass through a
  demonstrated opening to a witness target. Visual holes and blocking agree before
  and after the revision; the existing geometry guard does not keep removed cover
  protective. The adapter seam reports the same identified revision.
- Reset clears accumulated damage and revision state without leaving collision
  ghosts. Recheck ED-01 only where changed logic affects its prior passing evidence.
- Store identity (commit/dirty scope, map and actor, asset fingerprints, tuning,
  actual runtime/worker settings), focused hit/revision traces, comparable views
  and checks under `Saved/EnvironmentDestruction01/ED-02/<Candidate>/`. Include the
  consumer contract and exact open/runtime limits for MSQ-131.

Independent review is waived for ED-02 only by the scoped owner start. Complete
