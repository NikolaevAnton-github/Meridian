## Objective and dependencies

After [ED-05](ED-05.md), make the selected specimens and their materials communicate rifle impact
and local destruction through coherent visible damage, bounded particles/debris
and suitable existing audio. Integrate these responses with the established time
slowdown without changing the ability or weapon design.
The first specimen includes cladding and concrete. Basic irregular fracture
geometry, interior materials and large-fragment collision are already ED-02/03
requirements; this stage adds/refines material-specific chips, dust, audio and
presentation, rather than postponing those prerequisites until the final polish.

## Work and deliverables

- Reuse suitable project-owned/local licensed effects and sounds. Duplicate edited
  derived assets; record sources and tuning. Missing suitable inputs remain a
  bounded selection issue, not authorization for paid assets or services.
- Align impact position, effect orientation, exposed break surfaces, visible debris
  and sound with the actual hit and material. Keep holes and surviving geometry
  readable; a cloud of particles must not conceal incorrect damage/collision.
- Preserve the [current cadence authority](../../Approvals/CombatSlowdownCadence01-OwnerScope01.json):
  world, bullets and rifle cadence run at **0.25** during slowdown; hero movement
  remains **0.65**. Fracture/debris simulation follows world time. Declare the clock
  used for effect, audio and cleanup timing and preserve the established presentation
  conventions rather than retiming the whole game.
- Handle entry to and exit from slowdown while a specimen is already damaged,
  fragments are moving and cleanup is pending. Avoid duplicate impacts, impulse
  bursts, skipped damage, stuck effects or premature removal of meaningful support.
  Full time stop, force push and telekinesis are outside this task.
- Add effects within ED-05's representative layout and measure the changed cost
  with the same capture settings. Any revised physics/cleanup limits retain the
  established visual and collision/support quality.
- Any final dense/overlapping-break cost claim must remeasure the windows changed
  by final effects, rendering or affected physics. Otherwise label ED-05's dense
  captures as pre-presentation evidence and leave the final dense envelope pending.

  a coherent impact-to-break sequence at
  normal speed and established slowdown. Timing evidence identifies world/game

- One focused transition sequence covers entry/exit during active fragments and
  pending cleanup. Existing cadence and hero movement factors remain unchanged;

- Damage remains legible and shot blocking remains truthful with effects active.
  Effect/audio counts and new frame-time cost are measured and bounded at the
  representative density; reset leaves no lingering sources or components.
- Save short comparable footage with audio, timing/count traces, focused checks

  commit/dirty scope, map/actors, effect/audio and destruction package fingerprints,
