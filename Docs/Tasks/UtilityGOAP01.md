GASPALS/CMC. UG-00 removes obsolete active AI and leaves an explicitly passive/manual
intermediate. UG-01 immediately follows to restore basic combat. Keep only justified
primitives; no selectable legacy brain or old-navigation fallback. Git, source assets
and historical evidence remain preserved.

stages express order only; five mechanic integrations remain unstaged. No source
removal, build, editor operation or production dispatch occurs during task setup.

apply to future execution; owner gameplay/motion judgement remains separate.
