# OpeningEpisode01: playable exploration, recording and progression

Multica issue: **MSQ-80**.
Stage 14 of [CombatSlice01](CombatSlice01Plan.md).
Predecessor: [OpeningEpisodeDesign01](OpeningEpisodeDesign01.md).
Gate: owner's identified selection of the episode package and required asset scope.

## Scope

Implement only the selected episode beats in the retained lobby: bounded exploration,
the verified encounter, meaningful local interaction, viewing the surveillance
sequence, a readable next objective and the agreed progression endpoint. Build a
small reusable interaction path with range/visibility checks and explicit control
ownership. A vendor grab/push animation alone is not an interaction system.

Produce the scoped surveillance presentation from approved/audited available assets
following the selected storyboard, and label any accepted prototype substitution.
Support interruption, replay and return to play without duplicate mission events.
Use a minimal local checkpoint/reset policy, not a general full-game save system.

Preserve the selected information order and protagonist agency. No newly invented
commands, guaranteed escape, squad-death cinematic, new floors or unspecified inner
door function. Stop at the agreed endpoint rather than adding an unplanned level.

## Acceptance

- A fresh play session completes the selected start-to-end route with understandable
  interaction feedback, combat completion, recording and next objective.
- Out-of-order/repeated interaction, interruption, death and checkpoint restart do
  not skip required information, duplicate enemies or leave controls/time blocked.
- Seen footage, objective text and available actions agree with the selected design
  and fixed canon. No temporary developer diagnostics appear as final narrative UI.
- Reload saved packages and verify the affected route. Record actual runtime evidence,
  candidate identity, controls, sources and explicit prototype limitations.

Follow the parent plan; deliver `Docs/OpeningEpisode01.md` and the identified playable
candidate for independent review. Implementation does not self-approve story or art.
