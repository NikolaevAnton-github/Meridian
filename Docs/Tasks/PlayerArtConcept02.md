Preserve their original tactical design language: angular closed helmet,
opaque visor, shallow hard mask, graphite composite plates, restrained olive/
ash textile layers, athletic human proportions, uncluttered load carriage.
Add clearly greater armor coverage through credible thin articulated chest,
back, flank, abdomen, compact shoulder, forearm, thigh/knee/shin elements.

oversized plates, full rigid limbs, powered armor or exoskeleton. Each of ten
directions must visibly change helmet construction and armor architecture,
not merely recolor or rearrange tiny gadgets. Stay close to the chosen family.

Make the helmet computer believable via small integrated temple/occipital
housing, recessed service seams and protected visor display optics. These are
design proposals; no giant electronics box, exposed cables, external hologram,
glowing face, visible eyes or sci-fi power core. Helmet remains compact.
