# OpeningEpisodeDesign01: bounded opening episode design

Multica issue: **MSQ-79**.
Stage 12 of [CombatSlice01](CombatSlice01Plan.md).
Predecessor: [CombatIntegration01](CombatIntegration01.md).
Read [StoryCanon](../Design/StoryCanon.md) and [GameBrief](../Design/GameBrief.md).

## Scope

Prepare `OpeningEpisode01-Design01`: a concise beat sheet, player route in the
retained lobby, encounter placement, interaction/input contract, surveillance
storyboard, objectives, checkpoint/reset behavior and explicit start/end boundary.
Separate fixed canon, proposed connective staging and unresolved owner decisions.
Use a route overlay on the existing map, not a new architectural design.

Establish the player's knowledge through the selected initial context or local
exploration: the original mission, squad loss, unavailable command contact and
no accessible exit. These are prerequisites to the decision to continue; they do
not require inventing or staging the unresolved deaths, survival or catastrophe.

The recording shows soldiers carrying the wounded target upstairs, followed by
the discharge. Information remains local; no command contact or new order returns.
The protagonist chooses to continue the original capture-or-eliminate mission,
without knowing whether the target's death ends the anomaly or permits escape.
Do not introduce time control by the spatial antagonist, mind control, resurrection
or a settled outcome for the antagonist.

Propose a limited episode beginning after isolation; explicitly mark this boundary
as a proposal. Do not invent the squad's exact death, the protagonist's survival,
the wound's cause or the inner door's destination. If needed, present a small
concrete choice for the owner instead of resolving these through implementation.

## Acceptance and handoff gate

- Every beat identifies player action, local information, trigger, completion,
  assets and reset behavior. The route fits the existing walkable space.
- The storyboard preserves the canonical order and knowledge constraints. Identify
  how footage will be made from available assets, its prototype presentation and
  any missing sources; a placeholder is not final cinematic acceptance.
- List required new geometry/art separately. Any such production retains its
  applicable concept/dimension approvals and budget rules; no model generation here.
- Present the named package for owner selection of episode boundary and proposed
  staging before OpeningEpisode01 implementation. Record exact decisions without
  rewriting historical canon. Routine implementation choices need no extra gate.

Deliver `Docs/OpeningEpisodeDesign01.md` and the identified design package. This is
design work, not authorization to script an unselected opening or build upper floors.
