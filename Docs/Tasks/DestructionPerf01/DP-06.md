## Objective

Bound ongoing simulation cost while preserving visible damage and gameplay-important
rubble. Define sleep, retirement and wakeup behavior for the DP-05 pillar family.

## Bounded work

- Evaluate the installed Chaos Debris Optimizer as a candidate before duplicating
  its cleanup facilities; see [the installation/source check](../ChaosDebrisOptimizer01.md).
  Verify its kill/wakeup, query-only proxy and F6 reattachment limits against this
  task's gameplay contract. Successful module loading is not a lifecycle pass.
- Define per-tier active, sleeping, retained-settled and retired states with explicit
  transition conditions, collision/damage ownership and reactivation requirements.
- Derive low-motion/settling thresholds, lifetime choices and cleanup allowances
  from measurements. Document which timers use simulation time or real time.
- Remove active cost from settled pieces where gameplay permits; evaluate a cheap
  persistent rubble candidate where remains are needed. Preserve meaningful shape
  and blocking with a suitable simple proxy throughout each transition.
- Retire tiny cosmetic debris within its measured lifetime; keep gameplay-important
  chunks or an equivalent retained representation. Avoid synchronized mass cleanup,
  sudden visible disappearance and gaps or duplication in gameplay collision.
- Handle relevant secondary impacts and a second blast into settled rubble with
  explicit wakeup/activation rules. Prevent unintended mass wakeup or lost damage.
- Release lifecycle-owned callbacks, timers and transient components when work ends;
  make repeated transitions/reset safe and record retained resources.
- Supply DP-08 with the transition contract and candidate rubble requirements.
  DP-08 owns scene-level representation costs and any justified pooling.

## Acceptance and focused verification

- Use the DP-01 harness and DP-05 recipe to compare active, sleeping, settled and
  retired phases, reporting transition hitches and real-time frame/resource cost.
- Repeated blasts into fresh and already scattered debris do not leave increasing
  active-body/contact counts or leaked actors, components, callbacks or timers.
- A second blast and representative secondary impact follow the documented wakeup
  rules. Bullets, player traversal and cover agree with visible retained rubble.
- Check normal time and world scale 0.25/player scale 0.65; measure real-time cost
  separately from simulation progress. Confirm sleep, lifetime, cleanup, wakeup and
  reset behavior without using a smaller observed workload to claim budget success.
- Report p95, p99, maxima, excursions and counts across repeated cycles. Resource
  bounds and transition allowances require measured evidence; owner visual/play
  acceptance and the final scene-scale performance decision remain outstanding.

## Artifacts and limits

- Commit lifecycle settings/implementation, the state-transition contract and

  `Saved/DestructionPerf01/DP-06/`.
- Report exact versions/settings, measurements, evidence paths, focused check limits
  and unresolved representation choices for DP-08 in the task report below.
- Preserve source/accepted assets and exact baseline evidence. Do not erase useful
