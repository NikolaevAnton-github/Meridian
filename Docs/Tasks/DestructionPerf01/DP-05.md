## Objective

Develop a versioned representative NGD pillar family whose meaningful chunks
retain local destruction, openings, cover and useful contact shapes at measured
cost. Establish fracture and collision tiers for its later runtime lifecycle.

## Bounded work

- Select a representative stock pillar from the retained fixture; record source,
  derivative version, dimensions and comparison configuration. Preserve originals.
- Compare a bounded set of fracture recipes informed by DP-02: hierarchical local
  release, merging unhelpful microscopic pieces and simplified collision hulls.
  Preserve the visible silhouette, substantial falling pieces and contact shapes.
- Define structural/large chunks by blocking, bullet interaction and required
  damage behavior; do not substitute a visual-only result for meaningful geometry.
- Define medium debris with simplified collision and lifecycle requirements for
  DP-06. Evaluate one-way interaction only where mutual force transfer is unnecessary
  and the implementation is supported; retain required secondary-impact behavior.
- Classify tiny chips/dust as bounded visual detail unless a documented mechanic
  requires gameplay collision. Record that exception and its measured cost.
- Measure bodies, contacts and bodies released per frame alongside frame cost;
  triangle count and break-event totals do not establish active physics cost.
- Document per-tier collision/damage roles and proposed sleep/retirement behavior
  as the DP-06 input contract. DP-06 owns runtime lifecycle implementation.

## Acceptance and focused verification

- Use DP-01's fixed blast, camera, build and warm-run definitions to compare recipes
  against the unchanged reference, including intact, burst and active-debris phases.
- The identical blast still opens the intended space; retained cover and meaningful
  chunks are present. Bullets and traversal agree with visible openings/blocking.
- Check representative secondary impacts and repeated blasts; record actual body,
  contact and release distributions plus p95, p99, maxima and budget excursions.
- Identify which candidate reduces attributed cost and its quality tradeoffs.
  Derive any recipe limits from measured sweeps; do not invent fragment quotas.
- Record unresolved lifecycle behavior for DP-06. Technical evidence does not grant
  owner visual/play acceptance or establish the shipping hardware target.

## Artifacts and limits

- No revival of retired custom columns, lobby redesign or source-asset overwrite.
  Appearance/dimension changes remain proposals subject to applicable named owner
