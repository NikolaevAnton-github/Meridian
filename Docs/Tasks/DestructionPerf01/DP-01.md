## Attributed cost

In the first warm **light** trace, the tick-bounded burst region lasts 532.52 ms.
GameThread `SetPerParticleCollisionProfileName` accounts for **175.287 ms** of
exclusive CPU scope time; the enclosing break delegates account for 179.962 ms
inclusive. Do not add those nested values. GameThread `WaitForTasks` separately
accounts for **280.387 ms** exclusive in this region. Thus the profile-update
path itself is a material measured part of the burst, alongside synchronization.

During early destruction, the light trace records **224,739 collision delegate
calls**, 338.332 ms inclusive, and **194,931 profile calls**, 63.314 ms exclusive.
The active region has 262,826 collision delegate calls, 404.806 ms inclusive,
and 179,209 profile calls, 24.840 ms exclusive. Redundant notification/profile
work remains a concrete optimization candidate, but callback inclusive time
overlaps child functions and does not represent an additional physics-solver cost.
Call counts here come from native timer statistics, which retain quantized
zero-duration calls; reconstructed event counts retain positive-duration overlaps.

The installed engine already skips an unchanged per-particle profile value.
However, a changed value calls `LoadCollisionProfiles`, which walks the collection's
physics objects and submits a complete per-particle filter-data array. This is
source evidence in `GeometryCollectionComponent.cpp` (4792, 4872, 4882 onward),
not proof of the savings of a particular adapter. DP-02 should test batching actual
changed bone IDs per actor/profile, alongside reducing redundant Blueprint calls;
do not assume removing identical native setter calls alone removes the 175 ms burst.

The native task provider resolves GameThread wait dependencies. Unions of waits
at least 0.5 ms cover 281.41 ms of burst and 1,102.06 ms of early destruction;
279.74/1,102.06 ms respectively have recorded task edges. Those are wall-time
unions, not sums of nested or parallel tasks. Unmatched render/synchronization
waits stay unattributed. Worker interiors are tied to the awaited task's recorded
thread and execution span, rather than inferred from simultaneous activity.

The named trace makes the awaited worker work identifiable. One GameThread
288.215 ms wait points to recorded tasks `4653684` and `4653685`. Their actual
worker interiors are `FPhysicsSolverProcessPushDataTask` (256.542 ms) and
`FPhysicsSolverAdvanceTask` (32.281 ms). The former contains simulation-command
processing and recursive cluster-union lookup scopes; the latter contains
clustering work. A second 95.154 ms wait maps to 69.857 ms push-data work and
25.875 ms advance work. These are direct task edges plus same-thread interiors,
not guessed correlation. They are **named-trace diagnostic durations**, not
light/untraced budgets; recursive, nested and parallel times are not additive.

Early named tracing also records 1,802.113 ms of `Collisions::GenerateCollisions`
across workers and 969.951 ms inclusive in `FPhysicsSolverAdvanceTask`. Those
parallel/nested totals establish substantial collision/simulation work, not
2,772 ms of wall time. The light trace preserves the representative timing and
task graph, while some worker interiors remain generically named there.

The resulting DP-02 priority is: isolate profile batching and collision-event
fan-out first; then isolate particle/contact and push-data/transform work while
preserving the intended blast. Effects/audio/rendering/GC retain their own trace
rows and diagnostic observations, but the measurements do not assign all waits
to them or claim they are free. Field simplification and the installed debris
optimizer remain conditional later candidates, not changes made by this task.

The exact light-trace field-spawn engine frame is 154.87 ms at startup and
6.38 ms in the first warmed repeat. Later warmed burst frames still reach
171.12 ms. Preloading can address some first-use cost but cannot remove the
demonstrated warm hitch. The trace also contains a 213.68 ms frame crossing the
capture-completion boundary; it is flagged separately, because it includes work
outside measurement. Fully contained settled frames in that interval max at
11.80 ms. Neither the crossing frame nor serialization is relabeled as destruction.

## Budget implications

The proposed 8.33 ms frame target leaves only 1.95 ms above intact frame p50;
intact p95 already exceeds it by 1.92 ms. There is no demonstrated positive p95
destruction allowance at the current scene/settings. Keep intact, sustained
destruction and burst budgets separate. First reduce intact tail cost and establish
target hardware/scene settings; allocate sustained destruction only from measured
remaining headroom. Keep burst excursions above 16.67 ms explicit, without granting
a new acceptable hitch allowance. Production limits and the full stress matrix
remain DP-09/DP-10 work and owner decisions.
