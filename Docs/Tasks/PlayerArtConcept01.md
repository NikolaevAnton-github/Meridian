## Direction

Read Docs/Design/StoryCanon.md and GameBrief.md. The year is **2043**.
The protagonist is a trained member of MERIDIAN, the secret government unit of
parautilitarians. Realistic dark near future, plausible human tactical equipment,
urban interior mobility and rifle use. Their powers are time slowdown, force push
and telekinesis; do not invent a machine that creates them, biography, rank,
uniform standard, faction logo or explanation of survival as established canon.
All appearance details remain proposals. No existing franchise character copy.

Every design must hide all facial features and skin through an integrated mask,
opaque eye protection, helmet or cloth system. No open eye holes or visible eyes.
Explore meaningful construction and silhouette differences, not fifteen recolors.
Vary protection distribution, mask architecture, textile layering and equipment.
Retain realistic anatomy, mobile shoulders, clear hands/gloves and useful boots.
Avoid space marines, fantasy armor, glowing power suits and gratuitous gadgets.
