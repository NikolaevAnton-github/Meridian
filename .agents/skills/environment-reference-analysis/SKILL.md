# Environment Reference Analysis

Turn what is visible in the art into spatial relationships that an implementer
can build. The current assignment and owner instructions define the permitted
scope, accepted dimensions, and decision gates; this skill does not extend them.

## Read the architecture

Open the actual authoritative images at useful resolution before reading prior
interpretations. Identify the principal view and use other views to disambiguate
depth. Record disagreements between references rather than averaging them away.

Separate three form levels:

- **Primary:** room volume, main axes, solid/void distribution, colonnade rhythm,
  main openings and the focal composition.
- **Secondary:** portal depth, projecting piers, recessed wall fields, beam
  thickness and soffits, returns, reveals, stepped planes and terminal joints.
- **Tertiary:** panel seams, bevels, trim, hardware and material boundaries.

Trace a few decisive depth relationships in annotated crops and plan/section
sketches. For a colonnade, identify the front face, side face, space behind it,
back wall and overhead enclosure. For an entrance, identify the outer surround,
reveal/return, glazing plane, transom, door plane and transition into the last
bay. Do not interpret a dark region as a recess until occlusion, edge direction,
another view, or a plausible section supports that reading.

Classify each consequential feature as **visible evidence**, **inferred geometry**,
or **new design proposal**. Label source view/crop and confidence. Perspective
images suggest ratios and ordering; they do not provide exact hidden dimensions.
Keep human and camera scale explicit, with estimated FOV identified as such.

## Develop what the image leaves unresolved

Describe the reference's design language in actionable terms: where depth occurs,
how heavy masses meet thin frames, where repetition pauses, and which edges guide
attention. Avoid substituting adjectives such as "cinematic" for relationships.
Richness can come from a few substantial forms and purposeful quiet surfaces;
adding ornaments or panel counts is not a quality criterion.

Complete unclear joints creatively using that language. Prefer coherent returns,
thicknesses and terminations over disconnected surfaces copied from one camera.
Where alternatives are requested, vary a meaningful spatial decision while
retaining the shared visual identity; explain the gain and tradeoff of each.

Distinguish accepted primary dimensions and circulation from provisional proxy
surface locations. Use explicitly granted design freedom for secondary forms.
If a convincing solution conflicts with an accepted envelope, show the baseline,
proposed delta and affected clearance as a decision item. Do not silently change
the accepted geometry or flatten the design merely to hide the conflict.

## Handoff

Deliver a compact evidence-to-design mapping, readable spatial drawings and an
explicit uncertainty list. Use a common dimensional source for plan, elevation
and sections. Include human scale and a context view; a frontal elevation alone
cannot prove portal depth. When the assignment requires a named 2D package before
3D, remain in that phase and identify the package for the required review.
The output should let another agent explain both the intended form and why it
belongs to this reference, without reading the author's chat history.
