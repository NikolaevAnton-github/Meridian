# Environment Architecture Production

Produce architecture whose quality survives neutral shading and off-axis views.
Read the actual references, spatial brief and current task authorization before
choosing a production method. Loading this skill does not authorize 3D work: if
the current task is a 2D proposal or requires a design decision first, complete
that deliverable within its scope.

## Resolve the design before multiplying assets

Write a short assembly contract: accepted primary scale/circulation, secondary
forms open to development, essential reference relationships, unresolved joints,
and the views that will demonstrate success. An accepted room size does not
automatically freeze every blockout face. Conversely, proposed changes to an
explicitly accepted envelope must remain identified proposals until authorized.

Work from primary masses to secondary architecture and then tertiary detail.
Develop the portal surround and depth, column-to-wall separation, beam/soffit
thickness, end-bay transitions, reveals and returns before spending effort on
seams, bevels or decorative trim. Use plans and sections to resolve depth and
human clearances where the reference is ambiguous or the task requires them.

Complete unseen connections consistently with the reference's material and form
language. Show consequential creative decisions as proposals, not recovered
facts. Preserve breathing room and hierarchy: arbitrary complexity can weaken
the monumental or restrained character the owner wants.

## Build a representative assembly

When 3D is authorized, choose an assembly that tests the hardest connection,
such as a portal plus the adjacent bay. A lone reusable column does not test how
the hall is designed. Establish convincing depth and silhouette before repeating
the kit across the environment, unless the assignment specifies another scope.

Use modules around architectural joints and reuse opportunities, rather than
forcing the composition into convenient primitive bounds. Give visible surfaces
real returns and believable thickness; keep panel seams subordinate to the mass
they articulate. Check that profiles and junctions work from oblique and human
eye-level views, without floating trim, accidental slivers or unsupported ends.

Use the project's existing DCC, import and technical validators for pivots,
transforms, normals, UV scale, material slots and collision as appropriate.
Separate collision requirements from visual design; do not let invisible shells
force unrelated visible surfaces to remain flat. Retain editable source and the
reproducible assembly inputs required by the task.

## Demonstrate the result

Inspect a neutral, readable geometry view to expose depth, joins and proportions.
Also show the assembly in context using the authorized lighting/material stage;
neutral shading alone cannot prove the intended final mood. Record camera pose,
FOV and exposure so changes remain comparable. Include a principal reference
comparison and at least one useful off-axis view for the forms being claimed.
Do not replace a weak context view with a flattering detail crop.

Assess primary composition, secondary articulation and tertiary execution
separately. Diagnose whether a mismatch comes from geometry, camera, lighting or
surface response before editing. Fix the responsible form level; stronger normal
maps, more seams or darkness do not establish missing architectural depth.

Hand off the candidate identity, key creative decisions, acceptance conflicts,
actual images and remaining limitations. Follow the current task's bounded
iteration and review process. Installed skills, model choice and technical
passes are not evidence that the requested artistic quality has been reached.
