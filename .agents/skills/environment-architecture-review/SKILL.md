# Environment Architecture Review

Review the identified candidate against the owner's brief and authoritative
visual references. Preserve the separation between a review verdict and any
owner decision required by the task. This skill does not grant broader approval
or editing authority.

## Establish an independent first reading

Read the scope, acceptance criteria and reference authority, then open the actual
reference images and actual candidate drawings/captures. Record first findings
before reading the author's conclusions, claimed fixes or previous verdicts.
For independent review, work in a separate review context and leave the candidate
unchanged; place annotations and reports in the assigned review output area.

Confirm what each image shows: version, view, projection/FOV and relevant scale.
Match aspect ratio and camera framing as far as the evidence permits. A concept
camera reconstruction is usually approximate. Separate a probable perspective
difference from a demonstrated geometric mismatch; ask for a diagnostic view or
section when the available evidence cannot settle it.

## Judge from whole to part

- **Primary composition:** mass/void balance, human scale, main axis, focal
  hierarchy, bay rhythm and the relationship of the entrance to the whole room.
- **Secondary architecture:** readable front/side/back planes, column-to-wall
  separation, portal depth, reveals and returns, beam soffits, recessed fields,
  termination of the last bay, and coherent connections between those forms.
- **Tertiary execution:** seams, edge profiles, trim and material boundaries
  supporting the larger composition without turning it into visual noise.

Inspect neutral geometry or dimensioned sections where available, together with
the context views appropriate to the stage. Verify depth from an oblique view or
section rather than inferring it solely from dark pixels. A feature merely being
present does not prove its proportion, prominence or relationship matches the
reference. Richness comes from composition and depth as well as detail density.

Allow coherent creative completion of unclear geometry. Identify whether an
author's addition follows the reference language, is a documented proposal, or
contradicts a fixed requirement. Avoid enforcing invented hidden dimensions or
rejecting a meaningful improvement only because it differs from a weak proxy.

After this visual pass, read the author's report and inspect technical evidence
needed to resolve findings. Compare the result with the original visual target,
not only the preceding failed version. A corrected local defect can coexist with
an unresolved overall mismatch.

## Report a decision that can be acted on

For each consequential finding, cite a view/crop or drawing, state the observed gap,
its visual effect, and the smallest useful correction or missing evidence.
Use **PASS**, **FAIL** or **UNVERIFIED** for the task's criteria, separating the
visual verdict from dimensional and technical checks. Missing critical evidence
remains unverified rather than passing by inference. Do not average severe
composition failures into a favorable score because minor criteria pass.

For a bounded correction review, recheck changed criteria and visible regressions;
retain earlier unresolved findings explicitly. Do not expand the task into a
redesign unasked, or imply full acceptance when only a local correction was
examined. State candidate identity, scope, remaining blockers and whether it is
ready for the next authorized review. A role name, a skill, or higher reasoning
effort does not itself demonstrate artistic competence.
